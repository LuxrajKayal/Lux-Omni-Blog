This page explains how Luxraj Blog handles personal information submitted through the newsletter form.

Newsletter subscriptions and Brevo

When you subscribe to the Luxraj Blog newsletter, the email address you provide and your newsletter consent selection are submitted to Brevo so that newsletter subscriptions and communications can be managed.

We work with Brevo to collect your data and send you our communications. As part of this, Brevo acts under our responsibility and our instructions — we remain responsible for how your data is used for this purpose. Brevo may also, on its own side, use some of this data to help keep the platform secure (for example, to spot attempted fraud or hacking) — to find out more about this part, see Brevo's Privacy Policy.
