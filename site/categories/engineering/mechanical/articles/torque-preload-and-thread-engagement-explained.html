Torque, Preload and Thread Engagement Explained

See how tightening torque, clamp load and thread engagement affect the reliability of a bolted joint.

Engineering · Mechanical Engineering20 Oct 20268 min read

Torque is not the clamp load itself

Tightening torque is an indirect way to create preload in a bolt. Friction at the threads and bearing surface can consume much of the applied torque, so the relationship varies with lubrication and surface condition.

Use the specified procedure

Follow the design's fastener grade, lubrication state, torque value or tensioning method, and tightening sequence. A generic torque chart may not match a critical joint.

Inspect the complete joint

Check engagement, washers, mating surfaces and any specified locking feature. Where a joint is safety-critical, use the approved procedure and verification method rather than estimating by feel.
