Bearings, Shafts and Alignment: a Workshop Checklist

Inspect shaft fit, bearing support, alignment and lubrication before commissioning a rotating assembly.

Engineering · Mechanical Engineering22 Oct 20267 min read

Development sample. This page tests the Lux Omni Blog article layout and navigation. Replace this sample copy with verified reporting before publication.

Inspect interfaces first

Check shaft dimensions, bearing fit, mounting surfaces and housing condition against the assembly drawing. Contamination or an incorrect fit can cause heat, noise and premature wear.

Check alignment before operation

Use the appropriate straightedge, dial indicator or alignment tool for the machine. Tighten mounts progressively and recheck alignment because clamping can shift the assembly.

Record the baseline

Note lubricant type, initial noise, temperature, vibration and any measured runout. A baseline helps separate a new fault from normal operating variation.
