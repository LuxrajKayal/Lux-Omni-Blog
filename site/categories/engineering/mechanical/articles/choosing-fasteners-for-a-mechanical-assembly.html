Choosing Fasteners for a Mechanical Assembly

Match fastener size, grade, thread engagement, environment and joint requirements to the design.

Engineering · Mechanical Engineering21 Oct 20266 min read

Development sample. This page tests the Lux Omni Blog article layout and navigation. Replace this sample copy with verified reporting before publication.

Use the joint requirements

Identify the materials being joined, the applied load, vibration, temperature and available space. A fastener's diameter alone does not determine the strength of the joint.

Confirm grade and engagement

Use the specified property class and suitable thread engagement for the material. Avoid mixing incompatible fasteners or relying on a generic torque value when the design calls for a documented specification.

Plan for the environment

Corrosion, repeated servicing and vibration may affect material and locking choices. Follow the design drawing, manufacturer guidance and applicable engineering standards.
