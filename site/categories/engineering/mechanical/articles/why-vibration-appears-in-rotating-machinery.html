Why Vibration Appears in Rotating Machinery

Trace common vibration sources such as imbalance, misalignment, looseness and bearing condition.

Engineering · Mechanical Engineering19 Oct 20267 min read

Start with the symptoms

Record the speed, load, location, direction and operating condition when vibration occurs. Compare with the machine's normal baseline before changing components.

Check simple mechanical causes

Loose mounts, imbalance, coupling misalignment and damaged bearings can produce overlapping symptoms. Inspect only with the machine safely isolated when guarding or access requires it.

Use measurements carefully

A vibration meter or accelerometer can support comparison across locations and conditions. Frequency pattern, mounting method and measurement range matter; a single reading rarely proves one cause.
