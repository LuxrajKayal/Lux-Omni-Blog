Diagnosing a Drone That Will Not Arm

Use a structured checklist to distinguish configuration, sensor, power and safety-interlock issues before flight.

Engineering · Drone Technology8 Oct 20266 min read

Development sample. This page tests the Lux Omni Blog article layout and navigation. Replace this sample copy with verified reporting before publication.

Read the exact warning

Record the flight controller's arming message, firmware version and configuration before changing settings. A specific pre-arm check usually points toward the subsystem that needs inspection.

Check common system dependencies

With propellers removed, verify receiver input, sensor calibration, battery voltage, motor and ESC connections, GPS requirements where applicable, and any configured safety switch.

Change one setting at a time

Correct the documented issue, repeat the same check and keep a record of changes. Do not bypass safety interlocks simply to make the aircraft arm; follow the controller's documentation and test process.
