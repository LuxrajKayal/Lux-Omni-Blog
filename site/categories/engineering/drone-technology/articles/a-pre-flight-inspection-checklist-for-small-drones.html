A Pre-Flight Inspection Checklist for Small Drones

Create a repeatable inspection for frame, propellers, battery, sensors, firmware and the planned operating area.

Engineering · Drone Technology9 Oct 20265 min read

Development sample. This page tests the Lux Omni Blog article layout and navigation. Replace this sample copy with verified reporting before publication.

Inspect the aircraft

Check frame arms, fasteners, propellers, motor mounts, battery retention and visible wiring for damage or looseness. Use only components approved for the aircraft and propeller direction.

Verify configuration and failsafes

Confirm battery state, controller warnings, sensor status, firmware settings and the configured loss-of-link or low-battery behaviour according to the manufacturer's checklist.

Review the operating area

Check local rules, airspace, weather, people, obstacles and emergency landing options. Do not fly until the aircraft and the planned operation meet the applicable safety requirements.
