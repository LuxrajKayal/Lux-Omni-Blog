Choosing a Power Distribution Board for a Multirotor

Compare current rating, voltage compatibility, connector layout and monitoring features for drone power distribution.

Engineering · Drone Technology11 Oct 20266 min read

Calculate the whole-system demand

Estimate current for the motors and other loads under the intended operating conditions. Use the documented ratings of the ESCs, wiring, connectors and board rather than relying on a single headline current figure.

Check voltage and connector compatibility

Confirm battery voltage range, connector polarity, wire gauge, mounting space and the voltage required by each downstream device. A board should match the system design, not force improvised adapters.

Inspect before energizing

Check for solder bridges, damaged insulation, loose conductors and incorrect polarity with the battery disconnected. First power-up should follow an appropriate current-limited or protected test procedure.
