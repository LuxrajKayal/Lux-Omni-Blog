Drone Batteries: Current, Connectors and Voltage Sag

Learn how load current, connector resistance and battery condition influence voltage under motor demand.

Engineering · Drone Technology14 Oct 20267 min read

Understand the load

Motor demand changes with throttle, propeller, aircraft mass and flight condition. A battery's capacity rating alone does not tell you whether it can safely supply the required current.

Look for resistance in the complete path

Battery connectors, solder joints, wiring and protection components all contribute to voltage drop and heat. Inspect the specified current ratings and use properly rated measurement equipment.

Use conservative test practices

Follow the battery maker's charging, storage and discharge guidance, secure packs during tests and stop if swelling, damage or abnormal heating appears. Never short a lithium battery to test it.
