Voltage, Current and Resistance in Practical Troubleshooting

Use voltage, current and resistance measurements to frame tests and narrow down a circuit fault.

Engineering · Electrical Engineering26 Oct 20267 min read

Choose the measurement that answers the question

Voltage is measured across two points, current through a circuit path and resistance on an unpowered, isolated component. Selecting the wrong meter mode or connection can damage the meter or circuit.

Compare expected and measured values

Record the expected value, test conditions and actual reading. A voltage drop across a connector may indicate resistance at a joint, while an unexpectedly low supply voltage can point to overload or a weak source.

Change one variable at a time

Use the circuit diagram to form a testable hypothesis, repeat the measurement and document each step. Never move a meter lead to a current jack while leaving the meter connected across a live voltage source.
